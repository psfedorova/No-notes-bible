PRIVACY

Last updated 7 October 2026

Liber Arcanum is a book you write in, made by fedorova.studio. This page says what the book keeps about you, where it keeps it, and how to remove it. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is ever sold.

WITHOUT SIGNING IN

Your private book lives only in your browser's local storage on this device: the pages you write, your sound setting, whether you have seen the opening film, and a random mark this browser makes so each letter remembers which hand wrote it. None of it leaves your device unless you sign in or download a backup yourself.

WHEN YOU SIGN IN

You can sign in with Google or as a guest. Signing in is needed only to share a book or keep a copy of it online.

  • With Google: Firebase Authentication keeps your account's ID, name and email, as any Google sign-in does. In the book itself only the first word of your name is stored, to show other keepers who wrote a page.
  • As a guest: an anonymous account is kept by your browser, with the name you type.

What is then stored online:

  • the books you make or join, and the pages written in them, with who wrote or changed each page, the browser mark of each letter, and earlier versions, so an erased page can be brought back
  • the list of a book's keepers and what each one may do
  • with a Google account, a backup copy of your private book
  • a count of how many books you have made

WHO SEES IT

A shared book is read only by its keepers: the people who joined it with the owner's invitation. Your private book's online copy is read only by you. fedorova.studio does not read your pages.

WHERE IT IS KEPT

Online data is kept by Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). The page itself loads fonts from Google Fonts and the 3D library from jsDelivr; like any website, these services see your IP address when they send those files. Their own policies apply: Firebase, Google, jsDelivr.

REMOVING IT

  • Deleting a book you own removes it and its pages for every keeper.
  • Clearing your browser's site data removes your private book from this device. Download a backup first if you want to keep it.
  • To remove your account and everything kept with it, write to fedorova.studio and it will be done within 30 days.

YOUR WRITING

What you write is yours. fedorova.studio claims no rights to it and never uses it for anything but showing it back to you and your keepers.

CHILDREN

The book is not meant for children under 13, and does not knowingly keep anything about them.
